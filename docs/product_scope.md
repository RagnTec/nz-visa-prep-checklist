# Product Scope & Boundaries

This document defines the scope, user personas, capabilities, and explicit non-goals for the New Zealand Fee Paying Student Visa Preparation Checklist.

## Intended Users

This tool is designed for adult applicants who have already independently decided to apply for New Zealand's **Fee Paying Student Visa** (subclass for international fee-paying students).

## Product Scope

The checklist helps applicants systematically prepare and organise their factual information and required materials before submitting an application.

### Supported Capabilities

1. **Circumstance Collection**: Gathers factual answers regarding:
   - Study course dates, location, and Offer of Place status;
   - Tuition payment proof status;
   - Living fund arrangements (own funds, overseas third-party support, NZ sponsorship, scholarship, loan, or mixed);
   - Passport validity, health examination status, police certificate review, and study background;
   - Accompanying partner and dependent child visa pathways.
2. **Deterministic Checklist Generation**: Generates a tailored list of tasks and reminders based on pure rule evaluation.
3. **Structured Material Reminders**:
   - Identity and passport requirements;
   - Academic records, graduation certificates, transcripts, and grading scales;
   - Living expense funding evidence and supporter documentation;
   - Genuine study intention reminders;
   - Police certificate validity and translation guidance;
   - Family pathway specific relationship and maintenance evidence.
4. **Local Tracking & Organization**:
   - Status tracking (`未开始`, `准备中`, `需要复查`, `已准备`, `不适用`);
   - Progress bar and category-based organisation;
   - Dual filtering by necessity and status.
5. **Local Export & Print**:
   - JSON export for offline backup;
   - Clean, print-optimised layout for PDF generation.

## Non-Goals and Regulatory Boundaries

This tool is **not** an immigration advisor, decision maker, visa application portal, or document processor.

To maintain strict regulatory compliance and protect applicant safety, this tool strictly adheres to the following boundaries:

- **No Eligibility Assessment**: Does not assess whether an applicant is eligible or recommend visa types.
- **No Outcome Prediction**: Does not predict approval, refusal, risk levels, or processing timelines.
- **No Sufficiency Decisions**: Does not determine whether submitted or prepared evidence is sufficient or guaranteed to satisfy Immigration New Zealand.
- **No Tailored Advice**: Does not provide personalised legal, disclosure, or document submission strategies.
- **No Statement Generation**: Does not generate cover letters, personal statements, declarations, or explanation letters presented as tailored advice.
- **No Form Submission**: Does not autofill or submit applications to Immigration New Zealand.
- **No INZ Impersonation**: Does not represent or impersonate Immigration New Zealand or a Licensed Immigration Adviser (LIA).

When individual judgment or immigration strategy is required, applicants must consult current official INZ instructions or a licensed immigration professional.
